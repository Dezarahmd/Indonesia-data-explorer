Understanding Indonesia Through Data

Indonesia Data Explorer is a modern web application designed to make public information about Indonesia easier to access and understand.

By utilizing public APIs, this platform brings information such as weather, earthquakes, holidays, hospitals, universities, and postal codes into one simple and accessible application.
